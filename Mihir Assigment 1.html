About Steve Jobs

Who Was Steve Jobs?

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur, inventor and business leader. He was the co-founder of Apple Inc.

He helped transform the technology industry through innovative products and a strong focus on design and user experience.

Education

Steve Jobs attended Reed College in Oregon. Although he did not complete his degree, his experiences there influenced his creative approach to technology.

His interest in technology, design and innovation shaped his future career.

Career Journey

1976

Apple Founded

Steve Jobs co-founded Apple with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne.

1984

Macintosh

Apple introduced the Macintosh, bringing graphical computing to a wider audience.

1997

Returned to Apple

Steve Jobs returned to Apple and helped lead its transformation.
